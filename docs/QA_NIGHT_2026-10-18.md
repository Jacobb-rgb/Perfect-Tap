| Key | Pad | Action | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| E | X | grab (InteractController) | the Vault prompt moved to F for this reason |

No two systems act on the same key at the same time. H is not used anywhere.

### 2. ProximityPrompts

Every prompt keeps the default `Exclusivity = OnePerButton`, so when two F prompts are in range only the closest
one shows. The Vault counter has the only prompt in the vault.

### 3. Remotes and tags

- `OpenUI` has 7 client listeners, each filtering its own kind.
- `Notify` has 3: the HUD toast, plus the Sell and Tech panels' header receipts (those only while their panel is open).
- `Earned` has 2.
- On the server, no remote has two handlers.
- Several builders add the tag `Spin`, but only DecorController reads it.
- `Machine` and `Item` have several readers by design.
- No double consumers.

### 4. HUD on phones (static layout at uiScale 0.62)

The coins panel spans x 16–252 (design units) and the teleport bar is centred at 404 units wide.
- On a 667×375 phone the gap is about 80 units.
- On 568×320 it is 4 units.
- They would only touch on screens narrower than about 560 pt.

The toasts and the Zone label sit under the bar, and the Carry slots and the blast hint at the bottom don't overlap.
The Vault and Tech windows cover the HUD on purpose (they are modal).
Suggestion for the HUD / teleport bar owner: shrink the bar's buttons below 600 pt width.

### 8. Vault overdraw (fixed)

The phone performance audit flagged the Blaster Vault as the heaviest view for overdraw. It had 120 semi-transparent parts.
- Cause: 102 of them were the crystal shards. Each was see-through glass with a hidden neon core inside.
- Fix: `Vault/Kit.glassShard` is now an opaque glass column, tinted a little by the glow, with a glowing neon chisel tip. That is 3 parts instead of 4.
- Result: 120 → 18 see-through parts (the glass front's panes and two light shafts), and 2,969 → 2,935 parts.
- The crystals still read as glowing crystals in the vg_front render.
- tests/LobbyVault.spec.luau now caps the see-through parts at 24. Map v24.
- The 10 emitters already run at 4–8 particles a second.
- The hologram spinner (25 parts) is left as it is.
